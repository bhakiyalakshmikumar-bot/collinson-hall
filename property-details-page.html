A delightful ground floor retirement property for the over 55s, located within the heart of St Albans' vibrant city centre and a short, level walk to all its amenities. The block is served by a lift to all floors, and there is residents' parking available.

This charming ground floor apartment offers 517 sq ft of well-planned living space, complemented by a welcoming communal lounge and attractive gardens with seating areas for residents to enjoy. The accommodation includes a comfortable double bedroom, a spacious lounge, a separate kitchen and a modern shower room. With attractive landscaped gardens and dedicated residents' parking, this property provides an inviting, low-maintenance lifestyle in a friendly community setting.

Location summary

Beaumonds is set on Upper Marlborough Road, right in the heart of St Albans' vibrant city centre. Shops, cafés, restaurants and everyday services are all a short, level walk away, which makes it easy to get out and about without relying on a car.

St Albans is popular for good reason. The city combines a rich history with a fast rail link into London, well-regarded schools and a thriving pub scene, and the surrounding Hertfordshire countryside is easily reached.

The level approach to the town centre is a real advantage for those who prefer to walk, and the block itself is served by a lift to all floors. For anyone who does drive, residents' parking is available on site.

Residents also benefit from a welcoming communal lounge and attractive landscaped gardens with seating areas, creating a friendly community feel within easy reach of everything the city has to offer.
